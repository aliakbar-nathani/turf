Tips for Effective Time Slot Management
Create Consistent Slots

Keep your slots consistent across days to make it easier for customers to remember and book.

Optimize Peak Hours

Apply premium pricing for peak hours (evenings and weekends) to maximize revenue.

Offer Discounts for Off-Peak

Use negative price adjustments to attract bookings during less busy periods.

Consider Maintenance Time

Leave gaps between slots if needed for maintenance or buffer time between bookings.

Price Adjustment Help

Price adjustments let you modify the base price for specific time slots:

  • Positive values (+): Increase the price above the base price.
  • Negative values (-): Decrease the price below the base price.
  • Zero (0): Use the exact base price.
Examples:
  • Base price: ₹{{ turf.base_price_per_hour }}
  • +20% adjustment: ₹{{ (turf.base_price_per_hour * 1.2)|round(2) }}
  • -10% adjustment: ₹{{ (turf.base_price_per_hour * 0.9)|round(2) }}
Tip: Use higher prices for peak hours (evenings, weekends) and lower prices for off-peak hours to maximize occupancy.
